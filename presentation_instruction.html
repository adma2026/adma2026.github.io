ADMA2026 Presentation Instruction

In ADMA 2026, full papers will be presented on-site in both Oral Presentation and Poster Presentation formats. All other paper types (including short papers, poster papers, and encore papers) will be presented on-site as Poster-Only. Please prepare your presentation materials according to the type of paper for which your submission has been accepted.

1. Full Paper

  • Oral Presentation: 18-minute oral (15-min presentation + 3-min QA) + poster.
  • Poster Presentation:
    • Poster board size: 90cm (W) * 210cm (H)
    • Poster size: We recommend A0 size in portrait format; however, A1 size in either portrait or landscape orientation is acceptable.

Note: Full papers include those accepted as full papers in Research / Industry / Special Session tracks.

2. Short Paper, Poster Paper, Encore Paper

  • Poster Presentation only:
    • Poster board size: 90cm (W) * 210cm (H)
    • Poster size: We recommend A0 size in portrait format; however, A1 size in either portrait or landscape orientation is acceptable.

Note: Short papers include those accepted in the Short Paper track, as well as those accepted as short papers in Research / Industry / Special Session tracks.
