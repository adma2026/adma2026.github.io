Social Events

Welcome Reception - A Warm Start to the Conference

  • Date: November 13
  • Time: 18:00 - 20:00
  • Venue: Bauhinia Room, 3/F, Regal Riverside Hotel

The reception takes place in the Bauhinia Room, appropriately named after the beautiful orchid tree flower that serves as the emblem of Hong Kong. Guests will be treated to a selection of light refreshments, canapés, and beverages. Whether you have just arrived in the city or are winding down after pre-conference activities, this evening promises a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere to set the tone for the insightful days ahead.

Conference Banquet: A Cultural & Culinary Journey in Hong Kong

  • Date: November 14
  • Time: 18:00 - 21:00
  • Venue: Choi Fook Royal Banquet (Fortune Metropolis Branch)

This is not just a celebration of a successful conference, but a perfect opportunity to deeply experience Hong Kong's authentic culinary culture and rich historical heritage. We cordially invite you to join scholars and professionals from around the world for a memorable evening of inspiring conversations and extraordinary gastronomy.

How to Get There

  • Shuttle Bus service will be arranged directly from the conference venue to banquet.
  • If you want to get there by yourself, MTR is a convenient transportation way.
    Take the MTR → Hung Hom Station → Exit C/B2/C2/D6 → The Metropolis Mall → 8th Floor Choi Fook Royal Banquet
