Registration Categories and Fees

Each accepted paper must have at least one author registration. Authors with multiple accepted papers must complete a separate registration for each paper. Accepted Papers (Full papers and Short papers) that are not registered by October 10, 2026 will not be included in the conference proceedings.

The registration categories and fees are listed below. All prices are in HKD (Hong Kong Dollar). The time zone used for all deadlines is AoE (Anywhere on Earth): https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zones/aoe.

1. Author Registration – Full Paper from ALL Tracks/Sessions (Firm deadline: October 10, 2026)

(1) Benefits:
  • An electronic copy of the ADMA2026 Proceedings.
  • Access to all Tracks and Sessions in ADMA2026.
  • Access to all conference events, including keynote speeches, invited panel discussions, posters, tutorials, invited talks, the reception, and one banquet ticket. (Additional banquet tickets are available for booking on the registration portal for 1,500 HKD each).
(2) Requirements:
  1. At least one author of each accepted full paper must register at the Full Paper Registration rate by the firm deadline of October 10, 2026 (AoE).
  2. Registration must be completed for the paper to be included in the conference proceedings.
  3. If you have multiple accepted papers, you must register for each paper individually.
(3) Registration Fees USD/HKD = 7.8

ADMA 2026 Visa & Entry Information

Visa & Entry Information

All visitors to Hong Kong must hold a passport or travel document that is valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended period of stay. Please carefully review the visa and entry requirements below before making your travel arrangements.

1. Visa-Free Entry to Hong Kong

Hong Kong maintains a liberal visa policy. Passport holders from over 170 countries and territories can visit Hong Kong visa-free for periods ranging from 7 to 180 days, depending on nationality.

Eligibility Check & Requirements:
  • Check Your Eligibility: Please visit the Hong Kong Immigration Department Visa Requirement Webpage to verify whether you require an entry visa or permit.
⚠️ Important Note for Indian Passport Holders
Indian nationals must successfully complete Pre-arrival Registration (PAR) online prior to visiting or transiting Hong Kong visa-free (unless exempt). Please ensure you complete this registration well ahead of your departure.

2. Visa Application & Invitation Letters

If you require a visa to enter Hong Kong, we strongly advise you to initiate your visa application process at least 6–8 weeks prior to your departure.

✉️ Invitation Letter: Upon successful registration and completion of payment, a standard Invitation Letter and Receipt will be automatically generated and sent to you by the registration system.

Additional Information

🛡️ Disclaimer

The conference organizers cannot issue visas directly or contact immigration authorities on behalf of delegates. Invitation letters serve solely to assist delegates with their visa application process. Organizers assume no responsibility for visa processing delays or rejections.
