Camera Ready Instructions

Please be informed that the deadline for submitting the camera-ready paper for different types of papers are as follows. Late submission will not be considered for inclusion in the conference proceedings.

Deadline for submitting camera-ready paper:

Full Papers: September 11, 2026

Short Papers: September 11, 2026

Please follow the following steps in preparation for your camera-ready paper:

Step 1:

Format your paper according to the provided template (https://www.springer.com/gp/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines) and generate the corresponding PDF file of your manuscript. Authors should consult Springer’s authors’ guidelines and use their proceedings templates, either for LaTeX or for Word, for the preparation of their papers. Please ensure the latest template from the above link is used for camera-ready submission.

Please name the PDF file as ADMA_XXX_paper.pdf, where XXX is your paper ID.

Note that:

  1. (1)  Paper length:

    Full Paper: papers should be at least 12 pages and must not exceed 15 pages, including all text, figures, references and appendices.

    Short Paper: papers should be at least 6 pages and must not exceed 8 pages, including all text, figures, references and appendices.

  2. (2)  The paper title, author list, and author order in the PDF file must remain unchanged and exactly match the information in the submission system;
  3. (3)  The inclusion of the corresponding author’s email address is mandatory, and we encourage authors to insert their ORCIDs in superscript next to their names

Step 2:

Download the contact.zip (click here) and complete ADMA_XXX_authors.xlsx file.

Please rename the file by replacing XXX with your paper ID.

Please provide all the following information within the file, and make sure they match the information in the submission system.

  1. (1)  Paper type;
  2. (2)  Paper ID;
  3. (3)  Paper title;
  4. (4)  Authors’ names, same order as in the paper and signed License-to-Publish (LTP), separate names with commas;
  5. (5)  Corresponding author’s name, separate names with commas if there are more than one corresponding authors;
  6. (6)  Corresponding author’s email, should correspond to the information in the paper and LTP, separate email address with commas if there are more than one corresponding authors;
  7. (7)  Number of pages;
  8. (8)  Suggested abbreviated running head (should replace "Title Suppressed Due to Excessive Length" in the paper);

Step 3:

Download the copyright.zip (click here) and complete the License-to-Publish (LTP) form. The corresponding author of each paper, acting on behalf of all of the authors of that paper, must complete and sign the License-to-Publish form, through which the copyright for their paper is transferred to Springer. The corresponding author signing the copyright form should match the corresponding author marked on the paper. Once the files have been sent to Springer, changes relating to the authorship of the papers cannot be made.

Note that:

  1. (1)  All the information about papers, including paper title, author(s) full name(s) and order, corresponding author name, should match the information in the submission system;
  2. (2)  We do not accept digital signatures;
  3. (3)  One of corresponding authors should sign on behalf of all the authors of a particular paper;
  4. (4)  In the “Print name” field, please type the full name of the person who signed the form; do not place a signature in this field;
  5. (5)  Scan the completed copyright form in electronic format (in PDF) and rename it to be ADMA_XXX_cr.pdf, where XXX is your paper ID.
  6. (6)  Authors employed by US Government contractors or US Government departments may have to sign an alternative form. It is imperative that such authors contact their legal departments and also get in touch with Springer as soon as their papers has been accepted. If only a fraction of the authors of a paper requires an alternative form, then two forms will be needed for that paper. Please contact celine.chang@springer.com for more details.

Step 4:

Prepare your paper source files. Note that you should include all the source files, e.g., if you are using Latex, then you should include all the associated style files, special fonts and files for all figures, or Word files.

Create a folder named ADMA_XXX_source_files, where XXX is your paper ID.

Put all the source files in this folder.

Note that:

  1. (1)  Authors who use Latex template should ensure that the source files compile successfully without errors and generate the PDF correctly.
  2. (2)  Authors who use docm template should have saved this in the docx format, i.e., without macros. On no account should docm files be sent. Please ensure that the document can be converted to a PDF correctly without any formatting issues.

Step 5:

Create a folder named ADMA_XXX, where XXX is your paper ID.

After placing all the following files in the folder ADMA_XXX, zip up the folder and name it as ADMA_XXX.zip, where XXX is your paper ID

  1. (1)  ADMA_XXX_paper.pdf from Step 1;
  2. (2)  ADMA_XXX_authors.xlsx from Step 2;
  3. (3)  License-to-publish form ADMA_XXX_cr.pdf from Step 3;
  4. (4)  The folder ADMA_XXX_source_files that contains paper source files from Step 4.

Step 6:

Submit the zip file from Step 5 to the CMT system.
